About me

I’m a Product Designer based in Stockholm with a master’s degree in Industrial & Product Design and over four years of experience crafting intuitive, user-centered digital experiences. My career began with nearly nine years in academia, where I held management roles in public relations and served as the executive manager of an international design thinking journal. This experience shaped my approach to research, strategy, and human-centered problem solving.
Today, I design meaningful, accessible digital products that create real value for users while supporting business goals. My international living and travel experiences have also strengthened my ability to approach design from different cultural perspectives and create inclusive experiences grounded in real user needs.
Currently, I’m expanding my technical skills by studying Frontend Development at Hyper Island, combining my design background with hands-on development knowledge.
